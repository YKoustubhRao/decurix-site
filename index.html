The vision

Software gave people tools. The next software gives them workers: AI with a computer of its own, a responsibility, and the ability to learn. Decurix is the operating system they run on.

01

Train

Teach a worker the way you'd teach a new hire. Brief it in plain words, answer its questions, or record yourself doing the job once. Every answer becomes memory, and work it has proven becomes its own script.

  • Lessons, rules and skills it reads before every task
  • Learns from a recording, asks about what it didn't follow
02

Work together

One conversation per worker, on your laptop or your phone. It works on its own screen while you do yours. Questions and approvals arrive in one place, and anything that sends, pays or publishes waits for your yes.

  • A yes covers one action, never "always allow"
  • Watch it live, or just read what it did
03

Own

What a worker learns is yours: its memory, its skills, its record of every run. Each run is graded and kept, so your own work becomes the evidence a worker earns trust on, and the training data for a model you own.

  • Runs on your machine; share one folder, nothing else
  • Claude, GPT, or a model on your own hardware
How it works

Brief it like a colleague.
Check its work like a manager.

  1. 1

    Create a worker

    Click New desktop and choose its brain. It gets a full Linux desktop with a browser, office apps and a terminal.

  2. 2

    Give it work in chat

    Attach the files and say what you need, in your own words. No workflow builder, no integration first.

  3. 3

    It works on its own screen

    It opens apps, reads files, fills forms and writes documents. Every action is recorded with before-and-after screenshots.

  4. 4

    It asks when it should

    When a call is yours it asks a precise question with the evidence, instead of guessing. Your answer is remembered.

  5. 5

    Done means checked

    Totals, files, paths and recipients are checked in code before any model's opinion. Only a confirmed result is reported done.

  6. 6

    Next time, it's cheaper

    A job it has proven runs as its own script or replay, still checked every time. You're needed less with every run.

Principles

Correctness > intelligence
>> efficiency.

In that order. A worker may ask, or say it failed. It must never report success for something it did not see.

No unseen success

Facts a program can decide (sums, files, paths, recipients) are checked in code before any model gets a vote.

A yes is one action's

Send, pay and publish stop for approval every time. There's no "always allow", and no skill can switch the gate off.

Autonomy is earned

A skill stays an apprentice until clean, checked runs prove it. One slip and it's learning again.

The model is swappable

A smaller model with your answers should reach the same result as a bigger one alone. A smarter model just asks you less.

Your rules, in your words

Business rules are yours to set in plain language. The worker reads them before every task. No per-app hacks.

Every task on the record

Each task shows what was asked, what was done, where a person stepped in, what it cost, and whether the answer held up.

Your PC, your data

Workers' desktops run on your own machine. Share one folder if you like, and nothing else of your PC is reachable.

Secrets stay secret

Passwords are withheld at the source. They never reach the chat, the task record or a recording.

Where this goes

From one worker
to a workforce.

Every task a worker does is a probe: something to learn from and generalise. We think a worker that learns from each one, and proves what it learned, can in time take on anything a person does at a computer.

Now

A worker that does real tasks

Real work on a real computer, with you in the loop for judgement and approvals, and every result checked.

Next

Workers that earn responsibility

Practice on realistic cases, prove a whole process on evidence you review, then run it unattended within that scope.

Then

A team of workers

Workers that hand work to each other, ask a taught colleague before a person, and own whole processes.

Ultimately

Any task, learned

Your workers, your skills and your own model: a workforce that keeps what it learns and keeps getting better.
